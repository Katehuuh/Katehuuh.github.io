Labyrinth

Tilt the board to guide a metallic ball through a maze. Spatial audio ray tracing lets you hear walls, the goal beacon, and reflections -- fully playable by sound alone.

Arrow keys / WASD to tilt. D for debug. P to pause. R to restart. Gamepad supported.
