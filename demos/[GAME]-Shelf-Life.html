SHELF LIFE

Everything you ever claimed and never opened sits boxed on an endless shelf. Every crate is a world caught mid-scene, and you are the one thing on the shelf that woke up.

You fall from crate to crate. Each world gives you a few seconds and one thing to do, different every time: fit a power cell, ram a runaway truck, bark, pick a lock. Do it and the shelf files you upward. Do nothing and you drop a shelf, where it gets darker. A fall is seven worlds long, it always ends, and E on the shelf opens any world you missed.

Playthe best quality your graphics card holds
Needs
A desktop computer with a dedicated graphics card and Chrome or Edge (WebGPU). No phones.
Loads
About 220 MB before the shelf appears, then each world as you reach it (60 to 850 MB the first time, about 380 MB for most, kept afterwards): on a fast connection a world opens in 5 to 20 seconds the first time. The first visit reloads once by itself.
Keys
WASD move · mouse looks · SPACE or click does the world's one thing · E uses
Made of
44 worlds built from about 330 packs of a Fab library, most of them free monthly giveaways, staged in the packs' own levels. Built with Claude Fable 5.2.
